2. Collection and Use of Your Personal Information

We prioritize your privacy and minimize data collection.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your Personal Information with your consent or subject to the performance of a contract to which you are a party. You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

Your financial data resides in your device's internal storage (CoreData). If you enable iCloud Backup on your device, your app data may be backed up to Apple's iCloud servers, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

We maintain reasonable safeguards to protect the Personal Information we collect. While we implement these measures, please note that 100% security is not possible, and we cannot guarantee that the security measures we have in place will never be defeated or fail.

We may disclose your Personal Information in the following circumstances:
