Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 13, 2026

Thank you for using CreditStar. This application is developed by the CreditStar Team ("we", "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data through a strictly local-first approach.

1. Information Collection

We do not collect any sensitive personal information.

CreditStar is designed as a Local-First Application. This means:

  • All credit card details (issuers, limits, reward preferences) you input are stored strictly on your local device (iPhone/iPad).
  • We do not operate a backend server to store your financial data.
  • We have no access to your transaction history or card information.

2. Data Storage

Your data resides in your device's internal storage (CoreData). If you enable iCloud Backup on your device, your app data may be backed up to Apple's iCloud servers, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Permissions

To provide core functionality, the app may request the following permissions:

  • Network Access: Used solely to fetch the latest credit card reward rules, public promotional info, or exchange rates. No user data is sent outward.
  • Notifications: Used for local reminders (e.g., bill payment due dates) set by you.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not include any third-party advertising SDKs, nor do we sell user behavior data to third parties.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: support@creditstar.app
